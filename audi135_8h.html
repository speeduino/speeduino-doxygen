Detailed Description

Decoder for Audi with 135 teeth on the crank and 1 tooth on the cam.

This is very similar to the dual wheel decoder, however due to the 135 teeth not dividing evenly into 360, only every 3rd crank tooth is used in calculating the crank angle. This effectively makes it a 45 tooth dual wheel setup.
