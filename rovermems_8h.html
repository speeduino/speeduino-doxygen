Detailed Description

Rover MEMS decoder.

Covers multiple trigger wheels used interchangeably over the range of MEMS units including T Series, O Series, Mini and K Series

Specifically covers teeth patterns on the primary trigger (crank): 3 gap 14 gap 2 gap 13 gap 11 gap 5 gap 12 gap 4 gap 2 gap 14 gap 3 gap 13 gap 17 gap 17 gap

On the secondary trigger (cam) supports:

  • No cam
  • Single tooth cam (or half moon cam)
  • Multi tooth (5-3-2 teeth)
