Detailed Description

GM 7X trigger wheel decoder with six equally spaced teeth and a seventh tooth for cylinder identification.

Note
Within the decoder code, the sync tooth is referred to as tooth #3 rather than tooth #7. This makes for simpler angle calculations (See: http://www.speeduino.com/forum/download/file.php?id=4743 ).
