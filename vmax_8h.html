Detailed Description

Yamaha Vmax 1990+ with 6 uneven teeth, triggering on the wide lobe.

Within the decoder code, the sync tooth is referred to as tooth #1. Derived from Harley and made to work on the Yamah Vmax. Trigger is based on 'CHANGE' so we get a signal on the up and downward edges of the lobe. This is required to identify the wide lobe.
